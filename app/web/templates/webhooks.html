Webhooks

Send a small JSON event to a URL you choose when something happens: build.completed after a successful rebuild, review.stored when a month-close review is stored, and months.missing when the previous budget month has not been loaded. The event carries the month, headline figures, reconciliation status and a link to this dashboard; never transactions. That is enough to drive ntfy, Slack, Discord, Home Assistant or an agent that then reads details over MCP. Back to Settings

Verify a delivery

Each request is a POST of JSON with X-Ledger-Event, X-Ledger-Delivery, X-Ledger-Timestamp (Unix seconds) and X-Ledger-Signature: sha256= followed by the hex HMAC-SHA256, keyed with your secret, of the timestamp, a dot, and the raw body bytes. Recompute it over the bytes you received, compare in constant time, and reject timestamps more than five minutes old. Details and a Python example are in docs/WEBHOOKS.md.
