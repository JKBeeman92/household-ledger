Sources

Files in the internal format, and EveryDollar's exports (the built-in profile below), are read as they are. Any other tool's files are read through a source profile: which column is the date, the amount, the group and the item, and how dates and numbers are written. A profile is created the first time you upload that tool's files on Close a month. Editing a profile here re-reads every month stored with it (the files are kept as uploaded) and rebuilds. For a back catalogue, an optional month_from says where each file's budget month comes from: {"filename": "…(?P<yyyy>\\d{4})…(?P<mm>\\d{2})…"}, or {"column": "date", "rule": "mode"} for the most common month of the dates. The format is described in the README, under "Other budget tools".
