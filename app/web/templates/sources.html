Sources

Files in the internal format, and EveryDollar's exports (the built-in profile below), are read as they are. Any other tool's files are read through a source profile: which column is the date, the amount, the group and the item, and how dates and numbers are written. A profile is created the first time you upload that tool's files on Close a month. Editing a profile here re-reads every month stored with it (the files are kept as uploaded) and rebuilds. The format is described in the README, under "Other budget tools".
