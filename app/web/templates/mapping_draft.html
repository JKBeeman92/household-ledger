What the columns mean, and two things to check

Category is the budget group. Canonical item is the name the charts use; a year's suffix such as " 2" or " Fund" is dropped, so one envelope keeps one line. Flow says what the money is: income, expense, debt, transfer (a move between your own envelopes) or exclude (left out of every figure).

Check two things. Money you hold for someone else and pay on needs Flow exclude. A move between your own envelopes that the name did not give away needs transfer. Change the Flow in the box below, then add the rows.
